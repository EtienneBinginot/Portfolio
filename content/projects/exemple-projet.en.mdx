## Why this write-up exists

This section goes beyond the fixed fields on the card (problem, decisions,
numbers, chart): this is where the long-form narrative lives, the kind that
needs several paragraphs to lay out reasoning properly.

The headline number can be referenced here without retyping it — it comes
straight from the data:

And the before/after chart can be dropped back into the text, right where
it best supports the decision that preceded it:

<InlineChart />

The rest of the text continues as regular prose, with normal Markdown
formatting: **bold**, _italic_, lists.
